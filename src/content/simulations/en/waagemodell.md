## What is it about?

An equation such as $3x + 2 = x + 8$ is a **balance in equilibrium**: three boxes of weight $x$ and two unit weights on the left, one box and eight unit weights on the right. Anything that keeps the balance level is an **equivalent transformation**: removing the same from **both** sides, or dividing both sides into the same number of equal parts.

$$
\begin{aligned}
3x + 2 &= x + 8 &&\mid -x\\
2x + 2 &= 8 &&\mid -2\\
2x &= 6 &&\mid :2\\
x &= 3
\end{aligned}
$$

Check in the original equation: $3 \cdot 3 + 2 = 11 = 3 + 8$. If $x$ disappears, a false statement means **no solution**, a true one means **every number** is a solution. Negative solutions such as $x = -2$ cannot be shown with a balance.

Max writes $3x + 4 = x + 10 \mid -x \Rightarrow 3x + 4 = 10$. What went wrong? [Load equation](?a=3&b=4&c=1&d=10)

He removed $x$ on the right only – the balance tips. Correct: $2x + 4 = 10$, so $x = 3$.

## Notes for teachers

- Tapping an object removes it on one side only, so the balance tips and the line is marked red; removing the same on the other side turns it into a valid step.
- Discuss the limits: no negative numbers or differences, positive solutions only, dividing only when it works out.
- **Misconception:** dividing only part of a side, e.g. $2x + 4 = 10 \mid :2 \Rightarrow x + 4 = 5$.
