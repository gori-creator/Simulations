## What is it about?

If you know the rate of change $r(t)$, the total change is the area under its graph: $B(t) = B(0) + \int_0^t r(u)\,du$. Rectangles $r(t_i)\,\Delta t$ approximate it. Negative rates (water flowing out) decrease the amount.

*Example:* a car accelerating for $10$ s to $20$ m/s, cruising $30$ s and braking $10$ s covers $100 + 600 + 100 = 800$ m.
