## What is it about?

Sound is produced when something **vibrates**: the vibration pushes air particles, which pass the push on, so a **longitudinal wave** travels to the ear (transmitter – medium – receiver).

- **Pitch** is set by the **frequency** $f$; the period is $T = \tfrac{1}{f}$. Concert A: $440\,\text{Hz}$, $T \approx 2.27\,\text{ms}$.
- **Loudness** is set by the **amplitude**. Double amplitude: $+6\,\text{dB}$; double distance: $-6\,\text{dB}$.
- **Wavelength** $\lambda = \tfrac{c}{f}$: in air ($343\,\tfrac{\text{m}}{\text{s}}$) $440\,\text{Hz}$ gives $0.78\,\text{m}$. No sound in a vacuum.
- Humans hear from about $20\,\text{Hz}$ to $20\,\text{kHz}$.
