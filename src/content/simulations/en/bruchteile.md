## What is it about?

A fraction $\frac{z}{n}$: the whole is cut into $n$ **equal** parts (denominator), and $z$ of them are taken (numerator). **Expanding** means cutting every piece into $k$ parts: $\frac{3}{4} = \frac{6}{8}$ – the amount stays the same. **Reducing** is the reverse. Fractions can be written as decimals, sometimes recurring: $\frac{1}{3} = 0.\overline{3}$.

### Exercise 2: Compare

Which is more: $\frac{2}{3}$ or $\frac{3}{4}$ of a pizza?

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

$\frac{8}{12} < \frac{9}{12}$, so $\frac{3}{4}$ is more.

</details>

## Notes for teachers

- **Use:** Grade 6 (fractions, equivalent fractions, decimals).
- **Misconception:** "$\frac{6}{8}$ is more than $\frac{3}{4}$ because the numbers are bigger."
