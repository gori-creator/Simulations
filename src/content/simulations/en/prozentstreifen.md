## What is it about?

Percent means hundredths: $25\,\% = \frac{25}{100} = \frac{1}{4}$. Every percentage task involves the **base value** $G$ ($100\,\%$), the **rate** $p\,\%$ and the **percentage** $W$:

$$
W = G \cdot \frac{p}{100}
$$

Rule of three: $100\,\% \hat{=} 80$, $1\,\% \hat{=} 0.8$, $25\,\% \hat{=} 20$.

## Ideas for the classroom

*Just suggestions – you know your class best.*

- Let students estimate on the bar before calculating.
- **Common stumbling blocks:** mixing up base value and percentage, confusing the discount with the new price, rates above $100\,\%$.
