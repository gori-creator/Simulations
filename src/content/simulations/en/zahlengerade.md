## What is it about?

On the **number line** negative numbers lie to the left of $0$, positive numbers to the right. The **opposite** of a number is its mirror image at $0$; the **absolute value** is its distance from $0$: $\lvert -3 \rvert = \lvert 3 \rvert = 3$.

**Walking model:** a frog stands on the first number, facing the positive direction.

1. The **operation sign** tells it where to face: $+$ keep facing right, $-$ **turn around**.
2. The **sign of the second number** tells it how to hop: positive **forwards**, negative **backwards**.

This gives the rules $a - b = a + (-b)$ and $a + (-b) = a - b$: subtracting means **adding the opposite**, e.g. $2 - (-4) = 2 + 4 = 6$.

### Task 2: Temperature difference

In the morning it is $-6\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$, at noon $4\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$. How many degrees warmer did it get?

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

$4 - (-6) = 10$, so $10\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$ warmer. [Show on the thermometer](?scene=thermo&a=-6&b=10)

### Task 3: Explain

Use the walking model to explain why $5 - (-3) = 5 + 3$.

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

For $5 - (-3)$ the frog turns around (facing left) and hops $3$ steps backwards – to the right. For $5 + 3$ it faces right and hops $3$ steps forwards. Both times it lands on $8$. [Compare](?a=5&op=minus&b=-3)

</details>

## Notes for teachers

- The simulation links the arrow model and the walking model; for subtraction the arrow of the second number is drawn dashed and flips to the opposite direction.
- With `show=0` the result and the arrow only appear after **Go!**.
- **Misconceptions:** “subtracting always makes smaller”; $-3 + (-4) = +7$; “$-5 > -2$ because $5 > 2$”.
