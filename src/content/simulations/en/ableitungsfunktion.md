## What is it about?

At every $x$ the slope of the tangent is $f'(x)$; plotting these slopes gives the **derivative function**. Where $f$ increases, $f' > 0$; where it decreases, $f' < 0$; horizontal tangents give zeros of $f'$, and inflection points of $f$ are extrema of $f'$.

## Ideas for the classroom

*Just suggestions – you know your class best.*

- Let students predict $f'$ on paper, then check by dragging $P$.
- **Common stumbling blocks:** confusing a maximum of $f$ with a maximum of $f'$; confusing $f(x)$ with $f'(x)$.
