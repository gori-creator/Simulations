## What is it about?

A **family of functions** such as $f_k(x) = x^3 - kx$ contains one graph for each $k$. For $k > 0$ it has extrema at $x = \pm\sqrt{k/3}$, for $k = 0$ a terrace point, for $k < 0$ none. Solving $x = \sqrt{k/3}$ for $k$ and substituting gives the **locus** $y = -2x^3$. All members pass through $(0 \mid 0)$.
