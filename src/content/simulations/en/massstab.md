## What is it about?

A map shows reality **reduced**. The **scale** tells you by how much. A hiking map at **1 : 25,000** means:

$$
1\,\text{cm on the map} \;\hat{=}\; 25\,000\,\text{cm} = 250\,\text{m in reality}
$$

- **Map → reality:** map length **times** the scale number: $4\,\text{cm} \cdot 25\,000 = 100\,000\,\text{cm} = 1\,\text{km}$.
- **Reality → map:** convert to cm, then **divide** by the scale number: $2.5\,\text{km} = 250\,000\,\text{cm}$, $250\,000 : 25\,000 = 10\,\text{cm}$.
- **Finding the scale:** same unit, then divide: $3\,\text{km} = 300\,000\,\text{cm}$, $300\,000 : 6 = 50\,000$, so $1 : 50\,000$.

A small scale number (1 : 5,000) shows a small area with many details; a large one (1 : 100,000) a large area with few details. For tiny objects the picture is **enlarged**: at **10 : 1**, 10 cm in the picture are 1 cm in reality.

*Note:* the centimetres in the simulation are those of its ruler; on screen they may differ slightly from real centimetres.

## Tasks

### Task 1: Map to reality

On a 1 : 50,000 map two places are 3 cm apart. How far apart are they in reality? [Load task](?mode=calc&cq=real&cm=3&cs=50000&show=0&_hide=1)

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

$3\,\text{cm} \cdot 50\,000 = 150\,000\,\text{cm} = 1.5\,\text{km}$. [Show in the simulation](?mode=calc&cq=real&cm=3&cs=50000)

</details>

### Task 2: Reality to map

Two places are 2.5 km apart. How long is the line on a 1 : 25,000 map? [Load task](?mode=calc&cq=map&rw=2500&cs=25000&show=0&_hide=1)

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

$250\,000\,\text{cm} : 25\,000 = 10\,\text{cm}$. [Show in the simulation](?mode=calc&cq=map&rw=2500&cs=25000)

### Task 3: Finding the scale

6 cm on a map are 3 km in reality. What is the scale? [Load task](?mode=calc&cq=scale&cm=6&rw=3000&show=0&_hide=1)

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

$300\,000\,\text{cm} : 6\,\text{cm} = 50\,000$, so the scale is $1 : 50\,000$. [Show in the simulation](?mode=calc&cq=scale&cm=6&rw=3000)

</details>

### Task 4: Enlargement

A picture at 10 : 1 shows an object 2 cm long. How long is it in reality?

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

$2\,\text{cm} : 10 = 0.2\,\text{cm} = 2\,\text{mm}$. [Ladybird at 10 : 1](?mode=zoom&o=bug&z=10)

</details>

## Ideas for the classroom

- **Use:** grade 5, measures and scale. The “New measuring task” with the result hidden works well for pair practice; afterwards the class could measure their way to school on a real local map.
- **Model:** the landscape is invented; houses and map symbols are drawn slightly larger than to scale, as on real maps. Map lengths are rounded to 1 mm.
- **Common stumbling blocks:**
  - The unit is lost: $4 \cdot 25\,000 = 100\,000$ are **centimetres**.
  - Wrong number of zeros: $1\,\text{km} = 100\,000\,\text{cm}$.
  - “Large scale” is confused with “large scale number”.
  - Dividing lengths in different units ($3\,\text{km} : 6\,\text{cm}$).
