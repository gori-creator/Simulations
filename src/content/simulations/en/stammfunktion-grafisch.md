## What is it about?

$F$ is an antiderivative of $f$ if $F' = f$: the values of $f$ are the slopes of $F$. Where $f > 0$, $F$ increases; zeros of $f$ with a sign change are extrema of $F$; extrema of $f$ are inflection points of $F$. $F(x) - F(0) = \int_0^x f(t)\,dt$, and adding a constant $C$ gives all antiderivatives.
