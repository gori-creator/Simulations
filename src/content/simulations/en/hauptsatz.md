## What is it about?

The **integral function** $I_a(x) = \int_a^x f(t)\,dt$ measures the signed area from $a$ to $x$. **Fundamental theorem:** $I_a'(x) = f(x)$ – a thin strip of width $h$ has area $\approx f(x)\,h$ – and for every antiderivative $F$: $\int_a^b f(x)\,dx = F(b) - F(a)$.
