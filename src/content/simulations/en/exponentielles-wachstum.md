## What is it about?

Exponential growth: in equal time steps the quantity is multiplied by the **same factor** $b$:

$$
f(t) = a \cdot b^t \qquad b = 1 + \frac{p}{100}
$$

Linear growth adds the same amount each step instead. Doubling time and half-life are constant: $T_d = \frac{\ln 2}{\ln b}$, $T_{1/2} = \frac{\ln 0.5}{\ln b}$.

## Exercises

### Exercise 1: Water lily puzzle

The lilies double every day and cover the pond after 10 days. When was it half covered?

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

After 9 days.

</details>

### Exercise 2: Compound interest

$1000\,€$ at $5\,\%$: balance after 10 years and doubling time?

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

$1000 \cdot 1.05^{10} \approx 1628.89\,€$; doubling time $\approx 14.2$ years.

</details>
