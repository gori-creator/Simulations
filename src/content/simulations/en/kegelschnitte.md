## What is it about?

| Curve | Property of every point $P$ |
| --- | --- |
| ellipse | $\lvert PF_1\rvert + \lvert PF_2\rvert = 2a$ |
| hyperbola | $\big\lvert \lvert PF_1\rvert - \lvert PF_2\rvert \big\rvert = 2a$ |
| parabola | $\lvert PF\rvert$ = distance to the directrix |

Ellipse $a = 5$, $b = 3$: $e = 4$, foci $(\pm 4 \mid 0)$, sum of distances $10$. Parabola $y = x^2/4$: focus $(0 \mid 1)$, directrix $y = -1$. With one focus and directrix, $\lvert PF\rvert = \varepsilon \lvert Pl\rvert$: $\varepsilon < 1$ ellipse, $= 1$ parabola, $> 1$ hyperbola – the same curves you get by cutting a double cone with a plane.
