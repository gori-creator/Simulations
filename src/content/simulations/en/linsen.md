## What is it about?

A **converging lens** bends rays parallel to the axis through its **focal point** $F'$; the distance to the lens is the **focal length** $f$. A **diverging lens** spreads them out as if they came from a focal point in front of the lens. Three special rays locate the image: the **parallel ray** (becomes a focal ray through $F'$), the **central ray** (goes straight through) and the **focal ray** through $F$ (becomes a parallel ray).

With object distance $g$, image distance $b$, object size $G$ and image size $B$:

$$
\frac{1}{f} = \frac{1}{g} + \frac{1}{b} \qquad\qquad \frac{B}{G} = \frac{b}{g}
$$

For $g > 2f$ the image is real, inverted and smaller (camera, eye), for $f < g < 2f$ real, inverted and larger (projector), for $g < f$ virtual, upright and larger (magnifier). A diverging lens always gives a virtual, upright, smaller image.

## Tasks

### Task 1: Calculate the image

A $4\,\text{cm}$ object is $30\,\text{cm}$ in front of a lens with $f = 10\,\text{cm}$. Find $b$ and $B$. [Load task](?g=30&h=4&_hide=1)

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

$b = 15\,\text{cm}$, $B = 4\,\text{cm} \cdot \frac{15}{30} = 2\,\text{cm}$ – real, inverted, smaller.

</details>

### Task 3: Magnifier

A stamp lies $5\,\text{cm}$ below a magnifier with $f = 10\,\text{cm}$. Where is the image? [Load task](?app=loupe&g=5&h=3&_hide=1)

## Notes for teachers

- Thin lens, paraxial rays; rays bend at the central plane of the lens. In “Light beam and screen” every point sends light through the whole lens; a misplaced screen turns every point into a blur circle.
- **Misconception:** “Covering half the lens removes half the image.” – The image stays complete but gets darker.
