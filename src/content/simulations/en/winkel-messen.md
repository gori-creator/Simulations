## What is it about?

An **angle** is formed by two rays, the **arms** SA and SB, starting at the same point $S$, the **vertex**. Here the angle $\alpha = \angle ASB$ is measured **anticlockwise** from SA to SB, in **degrees** (a full turn is $360^\circ$).

| Type | Size |
| --- | --- |
| acute | $0^\circ < \alpha < 90^\circ$ |
| right | $\alpha = 90^\circ$ |
| obtuse | $90^\circ < \alpha < 180^\circ$ |
| straight | $\alpha = 180^\circ$ |
| reflex | $180^\circ < \alpha < 360^\circ$ |
| full | $\alpha = 360^\circ$ |

**Measuring with a set square:** (1) zero mark on the vertex, (2) edge along one arm, (3) read where the other arm crosses the scale – on the scale that starts at $0^\circ$ on the first arm. A **reflex angle** does not fit under the set square: measure the other angle $\beta$ and calculate $\alpha = 360^\circ - \beta$.

## Try it

1. Drag the arm SB and watch the colour bar of angle types.
2. Press “Random angle”, slide the set square onto $S$ and turn it at the round handles until its edge lies on an arm. Read off the angle.
3. Measure the same [reflex angle](?alpha=230&a1=10) with the set square and with the [full circle](?alpha=230&a1=10&tool=voll).
4. Play the [estimation game](?mode=spiel).

### Task 1: Reading the right scale

$\alpha = 40^\circ$. Leon used the wrong scale – the two scales add up to $180^\circ$. An acute angle must be less than $90^\circ$. [Show](?alpha=40&a1=180)

### Task 2: A reflex angle

[Load task](?alpha=295&a1=60&show=0&_hide=1) Find $\alpha$ with the set square.

<details>

### Task 3: Clock hands

Through which angle does the minute hand turn in 10 minutes? In 45 minutes?

<details>
<summary>Show solution</summary>

</details>

## Notes for teachers

- **Use:** grade 5, measuring and drawing angles up to $360^\circ$ and naming types of angles; the estimation game works well for practice.
- **Handling:** move the set square in the middle, turn it at the round handles or corners; it snaps to the vertex and the arms. With “Show the size of the angle” off, students must actually read the scale.
- **Misconceptions:** reading the wrong scale, thinking longer arms mean a larger angle, giving the other angle for reflex angles.
