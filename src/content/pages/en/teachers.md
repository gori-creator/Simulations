## In short

- **Free and no sign-up.** No accounts, no ads, no tracking. Nothing to install, no consent forms needed.
- **Any device.** The simulations run in the browser on whiteboards, projectors, laptops, tablets and phones – with mouse, pen or finger.
- **Works offline.** After the first visit all pages also work without an internet connection.

## Sharing a simulation with your class

Every setting of a simulation is stored in its address (the link). The **Share** button gives you:

1. a **link** for your learning platform, worksheet or presentation,
2. a **QR code** your class can scan straight from the projector (**Show large QR code** fills the whole screen).

Two extra options turn any setup into an exercise:

- **Lock controls:** students see exactly your setup but cannot change it by accident. (The lock can be lifted deliberately – it is a teaching aid, not an exam environment.)
- **Hide results:** equations, zeros and other solution values are hidden and can be revealed one by one.

## On the projector

- **Full screen** fills the whole screen while keeping the controls visible.
- Highlighted points can be **dragged** directly – also on interactive whiteboards.
- Zoom with the **+/−** buttons, Ctrl + mouse wheel or two fingers.
- **Save as image** exports a PNG, e.g. for worksheets.

## Curriculum

Topics are organised by grade and aligned with the German national educational standards (KMK). Each simulation shows its grade level and the related competences.

## Privacy

The site stores no personal data and sets no cookies. Only your colour scheme and language choice and – for offline use – the pages themselves are stored in your browser. See the [privacy policy](../privacy/).
