## As little data as possible

- **No user accounts**. The **contact form** sends nothing by itself (see below).
- **No cookies.**
- **No tracking** and no advertising, and no analytics tools in the browser. Only the host provides rough visitor statistics from its server logs (see below).
- **No external content** (no fonts, scripts or images from third-party servers).

## Hosting and server logs

This website (scitura.de) is hosted by **STRATO GmbH**, Otto-Ostrowski-Straße 7, 10249 Berlin, Germany. The servers are located in Germany. A data processing agreement under Art. 28 GDPR exists with STRATO.

When you visit the site, your browser transmits technically necessary data to the server, in particular your **IP address**, date and time, the requested address and details of your browser and operating system. STRATO stores this data in server logs in order to deliver the site and to keep the service secure (e.g. to fend off attacks). In STRATO's customer area we see summarised **visitor statistics** calculated from these logs (e.g. number of visits and visitors, popular pages). We use them only to see how the site is used and to improve it; we do not follow individual people. No cookies or scripts are used on your device for this. STRATO determines how long the logs are kept.

**Legal basis:** Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. Our legitimate interest is to provide the website securely and reliably and to have a rough overview of its use.

We do not transfer this data to countries outside the EU.

For more information, see STRATO's privacy policy: [strato.de/datenschutz](https://www.strato.de/datenschutz/).

**Source code and publishing:** The source code is kept on GitHub, where the site is also built automatically and transferred to STRATO's server. Visitors of the website do not come into contact with GitHub in the process, and their data is not processed there.

## Storage in your browser

Only on your device – never sent to us – the site stores your **colour scheme**, **language** and, if chosen, your **German state**, an unsent **contact form draft** (local storage) and a **copy of the pages** (service worker) for offline use. You can delete this data at any time in your browser settings.

## Contact form

The contact form does not transmit any data to us or to third parties. It puts your entries together into a message and opens your own email program. We only receive it once you send the email from there (the mailbox is hosted at STRATO), with the details you entered (name and email address are optional). Technical details (browser, screen size) are only attached if you tick the box.

We use your message only to handle your request and, if wished, to reply. The **legal basis** is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in answering requests and improving the website). The email is deleted once it has been dealt with, unless retention obligations apply.

Optionally you can report bugs, ideas and subject-matter notes publicly on **GitHub**. This requires a GitHub account and GitHub's privacy statement applies; name and email address from the form are not included.
