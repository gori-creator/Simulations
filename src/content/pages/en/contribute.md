## Open to everyone

Scitura is **open source** and an **open educational resource (OER)**. All source code is public on [GitHub](https://github.com/gori-creator/Simulations). This means:

- **Verifiable:** every school and data protection officer can check that Scitura collects no data and loads nothing from third-party servers.
- **Free to use:** you may put texts, exercises and images on worksheets, adapt and share them (CC BY-SA 4.0).
- **Better together:** anyone can report mistakes, improve texts and contribute their own simulations.

## Official and community simulations

- **Official simulations** are reviewed by Scitura for content and teaching.
- **Community simulations** carry a yellow badge <span class="badge badge--community">Community</span> and the name of their author. They are **technically reviewed** (automated tests, security check, code review) but **not yet reviewed for content**. After a content review they become official.

## How to contribute

| You want to … | How |
| --- | --- |
| **report a mistake** or send an **idea** | via the [contact form](../contact/) – no account needed |
| build **your own simulation** | with an AI assistant and our [step-by-step guide with ready-made prompts](https://github.com/gori-creator/Simulations/blob/main/docs/MITMACHEN-SIMULATION.md) (in German; the prompts work in English too) |
| **improve or translate texts** | as a pull request on GitHub ([rules](https://github.com/gori-creator/Simulations/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md)) |
| **spread the word** at your school | share the link, show it to colleagues – that helps most |

## What every simulation must meet

- **Only calculate and draw:** no internet connections, no access to data, no ads. An automated test checks this for every contribution.
- **Privacy:** no external fonts, scripts or analytics.
- **Bilingual:** German and English.
- **Any device:** from phones to whiteboards, light and dark.
- **Sound content:** formulas and terms as taught in school, with hand-checked tests.
